Frontier Survey

For nn unit squares, s(n)s(n) is the side of the smallest square that holds them without overlap. The table compares reported and verified bounds for nn up to 324. Of these, 81 have a proved optimum and 243 are open.

The best known packing and reported lower bound are the published values. The verified columns require an exact proof, algebraic replay, or rigorous certificate; external certificates must be replayed in full with their assumptions discharged. Finite-precision numerical checks do not count as verified bounds. ✓ same means the verified and reported values agree. Published proof, not read here and defect recorded identify limits of the verification.

The gap is the verified upper bound minus the verified lower bound. Polynomial roots appear as decimals, cut rather than rounded. Red marks bounds published or proved since 22 August 2026; Recent sorts recent verified bounds first. Records opens the case file.
