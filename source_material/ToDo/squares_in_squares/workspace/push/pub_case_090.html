    Claim and records
    Claim

    It is 1.72e-03 below the 10×1010×10 grid's 10, which the case holds in both lanes, exactly the 0.001722833074 the issue states; it is the first packing of 90 squares below the grid on record. Measured here it is a new arrangement, not one to one with the grid's 90 squares under any symmetry of the box by nearest centres: the optimal assignment moves 52 squares beyond a tenth. Since 90 = 10^2 - 10, it shows s(k^2 - k) < k at k = 10, the smallest k at which the record holds it; k = 11 to 18 (110 to 306) were already below their grids, and k = 4 to 9 (12 to 72) stay at them.

Scope

Square 1 of the retained witness (witness id 2) translates 0.000089 along (0.389073, 0.921207) with the packing still valid, so the configuration admits a non-trivial feasible motion; 17 of its 90 squares do. Every constraint is exactly affine in the slide parameter, so the arithmetic carries no linearization error, but the coordinates are the witness's own finite-precision transcription: this settles the retained configuration, not the true optimum. Rigidity and optimality are independent, and this bears only on the former.

Regularized drawing

Regularized by the Squares Project with Daniel's regularizer (evand/square-packing, MIT), with the project's modifications: port of his coincidence placement (regularize_grid_slack/2) rather than his code, same-side exact re-solve (devtools.regularize_same_side).
