## Key Results

- **39 wheels** loaded from JSON (single source of truth in `wheels/` folder), with **366 derived two-input spokes**
- **4 transducer wheels added**: gears/transmissions, magnetic circuits, piezoelectric transducers, thermoelectric transducers
- **Astrophysics and cosmology coverage**: W26 declares four sourced stellar/compact-object relations (blackbody luminosity, Schwarzschild radius, circular orbital speed and Hawking temperature); W27 declares four sourced expansion relations (Hubble law, critical density, Hubble time and Hubble horizon). Ten reviewed junctions connect them to the thermal, power, motion, density, gravity and EM wheels. W26's multi-input laws produce no two-input spoke; W27 contributes 30
- **22 ¢ primitives** (v1.0's 12 + v2.0's 10 new: exp, log, sat, hysteresis, grad, div_spatial, curl, delay, translinear, state_space)
- **22 legacy physical show-work proposals** with full 8-step pipeline trace (7 original + 3 v2.0 primitives + 12 expanded wheels)
- **22 legacy physical .cir files** written to disk (one per proposal, 9 SPICE templates); current transfer proposal bundles are under `examples/transfer/`
- **7 TIER 3 transducers** (Paynter, rotational-linear, C-L duality, electromechanical, thermoelectric, optoelectronic, electro-hydrodynamic)
- **Delta-sigma flagship**: √2 carried as bitstream, 36.9 dB noise suppression, NTF slope 28.0 dB/dec
- **Nine exact typed route modes across four operation families**: delta-sigma carries a value; the log-domain route computes a certified rational enclosure of √x; the op-amp + multiplier route solves a finite-gain algebraic feedback equilibrium; the KCL route computes an exact normalized sum or difference from two inputs; the fluid route computes an independent exact normalized sum or reversed-port difference at a declared pipe junction; the op-amp route computes an exact normalized sum or inverting-buffer difference from a matched summing amplifier
- **Three physical device bindings plus normalized KCL, fluid and op-amp sum/difference routes**: an exact RC/clock design for delta-sigma, a diode log/antilog reference design for √x, and an op-amp + multiplier feedback design with an independent loop-closure check, each with a conservative static error budget and an independent exact audit of derived quantities; the KCL binding preserves two exact inputs and audits the normalized sum or difference without claiming physical units; the fluid route declares normalized flow conservation but no device binding or netlist; the op-amp route declares an ideal matched-resistor summing block but no device values or reference netlist
- **Structural route audits**: every assembled route carries a `topology_audit` that checks unique component roles, contract port arity, declared parameters and exact-program wire coverage; malformed or unsupported compositions return an explicit `gap`
- **Typed wheel bridge**: the log-domain route’s ports map to W24 semiconductor quantities through a declared diode law, with an exact zero dimensional residual; unmatched text candidates remain explicit gaps
- **Method status and wheel navigation**: discovery carries each contract’s capability status and display label, method cards render it, and wheel-candidate pills open the selected wheel in the wheel browser; every vocabulary primitive is checked for contract, capability, dispatch and wire-kind coverage
- **Reviewed capability index**: all 41 exact contracts have capability tags and a provenance basis; canonical contract names and reviewed aliases are discoverable, and the tags describe exact operation semantics rather than claiming a physical route
- **Ordered multi-step goals**: `cent discover` splits sequencing words and punctuation into ordered `GoalStep` records, matches each step independently, exposes per-step candidate counts, and keeps the missing step-to-step handoff explicit instead of claiming a composed `¢` graph
- **Operation × method matrix**: `cent matrix` reports the declared operation-first catalogue without inventing methods. Every row names one evidence class — exact route, validated transfer, exact algorithm, structural template, declared bridge, inherited wheel candidate or gap — plus its role, domain, carrier, route and first missing physical model. Compute, carry and grounding rows are separate, so delta-sigma remains a carrier rather than an operation algorithm. The hits for `exp`, `log`, `mult` and `div` appear as four `validated-transfer` rows with the `log-domain-arithmetic` mechanism; they are executable reference-target handoffs, not exact route graphs. The envelope appears as the matrix's `exact-algorithm` row, not as a physical route
- **Ten-operation transfer corpus**: `cent transfer` declares ten operation contracts; `mult`, `div`, `exp`, `log`, `reciprocal` and `sign` join the original four. Each new contract names an exact or certified reference and a fixed test. Under D3, `exp`, `log`, `mult`, `div` and `reciprocal` retrieve the generated `log-domain-arithmetic` card and have independent target adapters; under D7, `sign` retrieves `threshold-comparator` and has an exact three-level adapter.
- **D1/D3 retrieval evaluation (honest, uncertified)**: `cent evaluate` scores every mechanism card and returns the top three structural candidates. After the five D3 recoveries, written provenance labels produce 15/16 candidate recall, 9/10 case recall, 2/6 negative-control failures, 14/14 reference target pairs and 14 artifacts; 15 unlabelled candidates enter triage. `certified` remains false because the sqrt/orifice label is still missed and two explicit negative controls still fire. The separate sabotage suite proves a constant score drops recall to 4/16 and target candidates to 3/14.
- **D2 generated Δ cards and executable Τ**: seven live cards are hand-authored after D7; `fluid-continuity`, `opamp-summing` and `log-domain-arithmetic` are generated by running declared operator chains over `kcl-conservation` and the abstract `transcendental-identity` principle. All seven operators expose `check`/`apply`; an unmet precondition is refused rather than silently applied. `tests/test_ablation.py` deletes the KCL-generated cards, rebuilds them from their interpretation records and reproduces all four fluid/op-amp sum/difference certificates byte-for-byte.
- **D3 log-domain arithmetic**: the `sqrt`-specific card is split from a new `transcendental-identity` principle. The generated `log-domain-arithmetic` card computes `exp` and `log` at the junctions, `mult` as `log → log → sum → exp`, `div` as `log → log → neg → sum → exp`, and `reciprocal` as `log → neg → exp`. `tests/test_log_domain.py` checks source routes, fifteen fixed vectors, independent target adapters and certificates; measured maximum widths are `1/92897280` (`exp`), `1/9814652388` (`log`), `1.2e-8` (`mult`), `4.24e-8` (`div`) and `2.94e-8` (`reciprocal`), all below the declared `1/10000` tolerance. The sqrt-specific cam control still refuses `exp` and `log` and names the sibling Δ as its unblock.
- **Experimental machine composer**: `cent machine` turns a complete function-tree concept into a portable `cent-machine/1` payload. The first machine, `sigma-delta-sqrt`, composes 12/12 required roles and selects its summing, quantizer and carrier parts from the existing transfer rankings. Its digital branch runs 100,000 one-bit samples to `1.414208989714491` (error `4.573e-06`), with `36.92 dB` in-band suppression and `28.02 dB/dec` NTF slope. The analog reconstruction/buffer, clock jitter and supply rejection remain explicit gaps; this is a side-arm, not a D-item or a measured-device claim. Assembled routes can be wrapped in the same four-stage template, with `--load mechanical` or `--load fluid` selecting a declared TIER 3 transducer branch; the load remains declared, not simulated.
- **D7 three-level sign**: the `threshold-comparator` mechanism ranks first for the `sign` contract and declares the exact law `-1` below zero, `0` at zero, `+1` above zero. `exact_sign_reference` passes three fixed vectors without binary float; the operation wheel is now 10/10 testable. No measured comparator, offset, hysteresis or metastability evidence is claimed.
- **D4 self-target utility**: `cent-self` (also `cent self`) emits two `cent-utility/1` records and a portable ledger. The Bresenham one-tick proof is promoted to the declared `quantized_carry_floor` contract; over 152 integer walks it agrees `152/152` where the existing two-tick target agrees `32/152` (`delta +120/152`). Runtime junction invariants on exact `cent-program/1` programs detect `12/12` injected structural and semantic faults where static `topology_audit` detects `4/12` (`delta +8/12`). Both records are `adopted`, carry an independent-oracle certificate with `candidate_on_certificate_path: false`, and their rollback is exercised by re-running the baseline path (`--revert`). The review ledger now exposes `adopted` and `reverted` states.
- **D6 retrodictive holdout**: `cent holdout` freezes five source mechanisms with publication dates before 1940 and tests five transfers whose targets were published after the cutoff: simulated annealing ← metallurgical annealing (1983), resistor networks ← random walks (1984), Hopfield associative memory ← Ising spins (1982), lattice Boltzmann ← kinetic theory (1988), and Steiner-tree computation ← soap-film physics (1941). The ledger normalizes the directive's arrows to target ← source and records the dated historical direction where the shorthand conflicts with publication order. The machine retrieves the expected source at rank one in **5/5** cases. One retrieved candidate for the resistor-network target is a declared false positive (`kinetic-theory`, statistical precision against exact-rational resistance); the rest of the unexpected candidates remain a separate triage queue, not false positives. A pre-registered **strict negative control** uses RSA modular exponentiation (1978): its required `mod` primitive is absent from every frozen source, so the machine reports `no_licensed_source` and returns no candidates; the control records a coverage miss rather than three structural false positives. The Bresenham ↔ delta-sigma candidate is **excluded**, because Bresenham's 1965 source cannot belong to a 1940 frozen library and the D4 correspondence is internal evidence. The corpus is deliberately small and hand-declared; the ledger states that limitation. See [`examples/holdout/README.md`](../examples/holdout/README.md) and [`examples/holdout/ledger.json`](../examples/holdout/ledger.json).
- **D5 Tier-D refutation**: `cent refute` pairs declared instances that share a signature but differ on a required observable. It generates **8 negatives** against **6 hand-declared** controls, including the exact Q-tensor invariant/orientation witness (`area 2/1` versus `66/25`), the two-tick/one-tick carry codes, current/voltage/flow junction carriers, sqrt-versus-arithmetic log-domain routes, and additive/multiplicative exact laws.
