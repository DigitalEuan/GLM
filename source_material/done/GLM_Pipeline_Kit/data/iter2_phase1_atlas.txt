Interpretation:

    This means the F_2 quotient 'sees' the transition as a no-op; the rule is
    invisible at the binary substrate level (Note 5's failure mode).

    The substrate cannot decide which octad to credit the change to. This is an
    escalation trigger — the substrate must lift to a higher layer to disambiguate.

    is at octads (weight 8 codewords), TAX = 8Y + 1 = 3.1174. A transition with
    TAX > 3.1174 costs more; a transition with coset_wt 0 AND codeword wt 0 is the
    identity (no movement).
