# Archived Rule30 Lean drafts

These 7 files are older drafts from Rounds 5-7 of the Rule30 study,
superseded by the clean Lean-4 files in
`src/lean/RequestProject/GLM/Rule30/`.

They are kept here for archeology.  They do not compile under Lean 4
(they use Haskell-style block comments, `module`/`public import`
keywords, and Lean-3 syntax) and several contain `sorry`.

Their content is preserved in the audit trail:
`Rule30_GLM_Study/RequestProject/GLM/Rule30/HISTORY.md`,
`Rule30_GLM_Study/RequestProject/GLM/Rule30/claims.csv`, and the
rebuilt `src/lean/RequestProject/GLM/Rule30/*.lean` files.
