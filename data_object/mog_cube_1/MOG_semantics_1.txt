Honest failures (what v2 still gets wrong)
1. Leptons don’t cluster. Ratio 0.982 means electron/muon/neutrino are barely closer to each other than to random words. Why: the charge+isospin signature dominates the Reality row, so electron(q=-1, i3=-1) and neutrino(q=0, i3=+1) diverge in the low 3 bits. Real physics says they are different — but linguistically “lepton” is a family. The encoding is capturing individual identity at the expense of family membership.
2. force = action = spin still collide (Hamming 0) — my Row-1 hash puts L¹M¹T⁻² (force) and L²M¹T⁻¹ (action/spin) on the same codeword because the anchor+weights happen to coincide. This is a hash-collision artifact, not physics. Needs a proper signed-exponent packing (e.g., 2 bits per dimension × 7 dims = 14 bits → split across rows).
3. Mass row saturated — proton HW=16 is at the Dodecad ceiling, mass itself HW=6. The log2(m_eV) ladder compresses 100 orders of magnitude into 3 bits, which is too coarse for baryons vs leptons.
