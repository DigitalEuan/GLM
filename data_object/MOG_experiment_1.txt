## 2. EXPERIMENTAL METHOD: Causal Attention Head Ablation

**The Technique:**
1. Identify "pre-emergence" checkpoint (before capability appears)
2. Identify "post-emergence" checkpoint (after capability appears)
3. For each attention head:
   - Patch the **post-emergence attention pattern** into the pre-emergence model
   - Measure change in performance
4. Select top-K heads with largest performance change

**For your UBP/GLM:**
Apply this to your MOG-mind:
- **Pre-emergence**: Mind fails on a specific ARC task
- **Post-emergence**: Mind solves it
- **Patch**: Replace specific MOG channels or driving styles from post-emergence into pre-emergence
- **Identify**: Which semantic channels (Mass/Info/Activation/Potential) are causal?
