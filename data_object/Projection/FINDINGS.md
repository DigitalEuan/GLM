# Findings of the projection sub-study

Every entry is a Lean theorem in this directory: no `sorry`, no added axiom.
The verdict tags are:

* **Proved** — a theorem, unconditionally.
* **Proved (cond.)** — a theorem, with a classical transcendence result carried
  as an explicit hypothesis (the pinned Mathlib does not contain it).
* **Correction** — the framework's version of the claim is wrong; the corrected
  version is the one proved.
* **Out of reach** — genuine mathematics that nobody can prove today, or a claim
  of a kind that cannot be proved at all.

Theorem names are in namespace `UBPProjection` unless stated otherwise.

| L-8 | `φ = 2 cos(π/5)`. | `phi_eq_two_cos` | Proved |

under everything except the analytic layer.  `e` cannot come from the lattice; it
must come from the flow.  `φ` can and does come from the lattice layer — as a
character value.

## Module 2 — `OneParameter.lean`: the three motions

| # | statement | theorem | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| P2-1 | The rotations form a one-parameter group; `rot t ^ n = rot (n t)`. | `rot_mul`, `rot_pow` | Proved |
| P2-2 | **`π` generates the period lattice**: `rot t = 1 ↔ t ∈ 2πℤ`. | `rot_eq_one_iff`, `period_lattice_eq` | Proved |
| P2-3 | `2π` is the least positive period. | `two_pi_least_period` | Proved |
| P2-4 | The fibre over "the rotation closed up" is infinite (the winding number is lost). | `period_fibre_infinite` | Proved |
| P2-5 | A shear moves every vector linearly in the number of steps. | `shear_pow_mulVec`, `shear_unipotent` | Proved |
| P2-6 | The only eigenvalue of a shear is `1`; no shear has `φ` as an eigenvalue. | `shear_eigenvalue_eq_one` | **Correction** |
| P2-7 | `φ` is an eigenvalue of the Fibonacci matrix (a *stretch*). | `fibMat_eigenvector`, `fibMat_pow_mulVec` | Proved |
| P2-8 | The Galois conjugate `1 − φ = −1/φ` is the second eigenvalue. | `fibMat_conj_eigenvector`, `phi_conj_eq` | Proved |
| P2-9 | The eigenvalue fibre of `φ` is **finite, of size 2**: `{x : x² = x+1} = {φ, 1−φ}`. | `quadratic_eigenvalue_fibre` | Proved |
| P2-10 | Exponential beats linear: stretch and shear are genuinely different motions. | `phi_pow_beats_linear` | Proved |
| P2-11 | A determinant-1 motion has a real eigenvalue iff `|trace| ≥ 2`. | `sl2_real_eigenvalue_iff` | Proved |
| P2-12 | The elliptic / parabolic / hyperbolic trichotomy. | `sl2_trichotomy` | Proved |
| P2-13 | **`e` is the time-1 value of the flow generated by the identity vector field**, and this determines it. | `flow_time_one` | Proved |
| P2-14 | The fibre of `e` as a flow value is the whole time axis. | `e_flow_fibre` | Proved |

**Reading.** `φ` is an eigenvalue (finite data: a rank-2 lattice and one integer
matrix, fibre of size 2); `π` is a period (needs a compact flow, fibre `ℤ`); `e`
is a flow time (needs a non-compact flow, fibre `ℝ`).  The framework's
"φ shears, π rotates, e counts" is right in spirit; the correction is that `φ`'s
motion is a **stretch**, and the difference between a stretch and a shear is
P2-6 versus P2-7.

## Module 3 — `Fibre.lean`: what a single number forgets

| # | statement | theorem | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| P3-1 | The trace is a class function with an **infinite** fibre over `2`. | `trace_conj_eq`, `trace_fibre_infinite` | Proved |
| P3-2 | Same trace, same determinant, same characteristic polynomial — and **not conjugate over `ℤ`**. | `same_trace_not_conjugate` | Proved |
| P3-3 | The fibre of `⌊·⌋` over `13` is `[13,14)`, of measure 1. | `floor_fibre_thirteen`, `floor_fibre_measure` | Proved |
| P3-4 | Three different seed monomials have hull `13`: `πφe`, `πφ³`, `π⁴/e²`. | `three_monomials_give_thirteen` | Proved |
| P3-5 | **The hull cannot be run backwards.** | `hull_map_not_injective`, `thirteen_not_invertible` | Proved |

**Reading.** This is the exact version of "you need several cubes and you must
move them": the number you measure is a projection, and the fibre over it
contains genuinely different motions.  Applied to the framework's own `13`, it
says the seed-recovery programme is not merely unsolved but impossible.

## Module 4 — `Cheapest.lean`: in what sense `φ` is cheapest

| # | statement | theorem | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| P4-1 | `φ` is a quadratic Pisot number (`x² − x − 1`, conjugate of modulus `0.618…`). | `phi_isQuadPisot` | Proved |
| P4-2 | **No quadratic Pisot number is smaller than `φ`.** | `quadratic_pisot_ge_phi` | Proved |
| P4-3 | The plastic number `ρ` (root of `x³ = x+1`) is **smaller** than `φ`. | `plastic_lt_phi`, `plastic_cubic`, `plastic_bounds` | **Correction** |
| P4-4 | `ρ` is a Pisot number: both conjugates have modulus `√(ρ²−1) < 1`. | `plastic_conjugates_inside_disc` | Proved |
| P4-5 | **`φ` is badly approximable**: `|φ − p/q| ≥ 1/(3q²)` for every rational. | `phi_badly_approximable` | Proved |
| P4-6 | `φ` is not a Liouville number. | `phi_not_liouville` | Proved |

**Reading.** "The cheapest self-similarity an integer lattice supports" is true
in dimension 2 and false in general.  And the property responsible for `φ`'s
appearance in packing and stability arguments is P4-5, not self-similarity; the
two should not be conflated.  (The sharp constant in P4-5 is `1/(√5 q²)` —
Hurwitz's theorem — which is not formalised here; the constant `1/3` is a safe
substitute and carries the same qualitative content.)

## Appendix — `Independence.lean`: the two branches of `trdeg ℚ(π,e)`

| # | statement | theorem | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-1 | `π e` is either transcendental or algebraic: the two branches are exhaustive, there is no third option. | `pi_mul_e_dichotomy` | Proved |
| A-2 | **Branch B**: a rational monad forces `π e` to be algebraic — an algebraic relation between `π` and `e`.  Independence and a rational `ℳ` cannot both hold. | `pi_mul_e_isAlgebraic_of_monad_rat` | Proved |
| A-3 | **Branch A**: if `π e` is transcendental then `ℳ` is irrational. | `monad_irrational_of_pi_mul_e_transcendental` | Proved |
| A-4 | And then `w` and `L` are irrational too. | `wobble_irrational_of_pi_mul_e_transcendental` | Proved |

**Reading.** Which branch holds is open; both are formalised conditionally, and
neither is asserted.  The transcendence degree of `ℚ(π,e)` over `ℚ` is 1 or 2 as
a cardinal — the suggestion that there might be a third option is not available.

## Stated out of reach, up front

| claim | why |
|---|---|
| Algebraic independence of `π` and `e`; `trdeg ℚ(π,e) = 2`. | Open problem.  The transcendence degree is 1 or 2 as a cardinal; there is no third option.  Both branches can be carried conditionally, and the framework's minimality claim depends on which holds. |
| "These are the *required* higher-dimensional attributes." | Sufficiency of a modelling choice is provable; necessity never is. |
| The icosian construction of the Leech lattice in full. | Very large.  The consequence the framework wants — a `ℤ[φ]`-module structure in which multiplication by `φ` acts as a similarity — can be carried as a hypothesis and its consequences derived. |
| "Meaning", "Time", "resonance" as physics. | Not mathematics.  As *defined cost functions* they are formalised (Module 5); as physical claims they are outside what a proof assistant can address. |
| Irrationality of `ℳ = πφe`, `w`, `L`. | Follows from algebraic independence; open, as recorded in the parent study. |
